# Next Video, autoplay, and confetti are player settings

At the end of a Video, the next Video is the next in the Session, or the first Video of the next Session. The player plays that next Video. Autoplay, confetti on Watched, speed, and subtitle visibility are settings (defaults: autoplay on, confetti off, remember speed). Changing Video does not exit player fullscreen. These are not Library concepts; they must not be confused with Captioning progress or Watched.
